Web Historia — The Free, Open-Source Pax Historia Alternative

Web Historia is a free, open-source AI grand strategy game that runs entirely in your browser, built on the open-source Open Historia codebase. Same idea — lead a nation, act in plain language, let an AI simulate the world — but free, open source, and with no install: open a tab, bring your own AI key, play. No purchase, no subscription, no metering.

What the Two Games Share

Pax Historia is a commercial alternate-history sandbox played in the browser, where an AI simulates how nations respond to the actions players type in natural language; players start diplomatic chats, jump forward in time, and publish custom maps and presets for others to play. Web Historia was started by fans of exactly that loop who wanted it to be:

  • Free, with no metering — no tokens, no subscription, no daily limit on how long you play. The only cost is whatever your AI provider charges, and OpenRouter has free models that cost nothing.
  • Open source — the full codebase is on GitHub under the AGPL-3.0 license, and contributions shape the game.
  • Playable anywhere — a pure web app. No download, no install: it runs in any modern browser on Windows, macOS, Linux, or ChromeOS, and saves stay in your browser.
  • Bring-your-own AI — one OpenRouter key gives you a curated catalog of models, from free budget models to frontier reasoning models, with recommended defaults per task.
  • A real map editor — a full vector editor to draw regions, redraw borders, paint owners, and place cities, with a Scenario Hub to share the result.

Also a Free Alternative to Age of History 3

Web Historia is also a free alternative to Age of History 3. Like Age of History, it features territory-based nation management, troop deployment, and historical scenarios. What it adds:

  • AI-driven simulation: you act in plain language and the AI writes what happens next, instead of fixed mechanics
  • Natural-language negotiation with every nation's leader
  • An AI advisor for situation briefings and strategic advice
  • A community Scenario Hub for sharing and discovering player-made worlds
  • Full vector map editing built directly into the game

If you are looking for games like Age of History 3 but free, Web Historia is the open-source option.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Web Historia really free?

Yes. Web Historia is completely free under the AGPL-3.0 license: no purchase, no microtransactions, no subscription, no ads, no play-time limits. The one thing you supply is an AI provider — OpenRouter offers free models, and paid providers charge you directly for what you use.

Is Web Historia the same as Pax Historia?

No. Web Historia is an independent, open-source project inspired by the same idea — AI-simulated alternate history — and is not affiliated with Pax Historia. It builds on the open-source Open Historia codebase and is maintained by its community for players who want that experience free, in the browser, with their own AI, and with a full map editor.

Do I need to download anything?

No. Web Historia is a pure web app — it runs entirely in your browser. Your saves are stored in the browser, and the world map streams from community content nodes, so there is no server to run and nothing to install.

What's the relationship to Open Historia?

Web Historia is a fork of Open Historia, an open-source project that shares its vision. We use its code, map data, and art assets, and we give back under the same AGPL-3.0 license. Web Historia focuses on the browser experience: a cleaner interface, better AI routing, and deeper systems — economy, war, policies, diplomacy, technology, and ideology.

Can I create and share my own scenarios?

Yes. Use the full vector map editor to draw regions, place cities, set owners, and define starting conditions, then publish the scenario to the Scenario Hub from the in-game Community tab.
